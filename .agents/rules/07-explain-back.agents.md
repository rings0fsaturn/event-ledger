# Explain Back

- A core task or phase is not complete until the author explains the mechanism in their own words.
- Confirm what is correct and identify only the missing or incorrect parts.
- Prefer a guiding question that helps the author derive the answer.
- Ask for re-derivation when a key concept is not understood; do not accept memorized statements.
- The author should be able to explain both **what happens** and **why the design works**.
- They should also be able to execute the next step and recognize success or failure.
